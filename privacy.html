Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

At FOBS Subtitles (hosted at tanvir210111.github.io/FOBS-Team), we are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our visitors. This is a static informational website operated by FOBS Team.

2. Information Collection & Cookies

FOBS Subtitles does not collect personal information, require user registration, or operate any user-tracking database. We do not use tracking cookies or sell visitor analytics.

3. Third-Party Services & Embeds

Our pages contain external links and embeds to third-party services:

  • YouTube (Trailers): Video previews are embedded using YouTube's standard privacy-respecting embeds.
  • Telegram & WhatsApp: External links leading directly to official community channels.
  • Subsource / External Subtitle Hosts: Direct download links to subtitle files.
  • TMDB / IMDb / MDL: Reference image and metadata links.

These third-party platforms have their own independent privacy policies, and FOBS Team does not control their data handling.

4. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or join our Telegram Channel.
