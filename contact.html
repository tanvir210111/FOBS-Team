Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I load a Bangla subtitle (.srt) on my PC or Mobile?

In VLC, MX Player, or KMPlayer, simply keep the video file and subtitle file in the same folder with the exact same name, or drag and drop the `.srt` file directly into the player window.

Q: The subtitle is slightly faster or slower than the video. What should I do?

In VLC media player, use shortcut keys G or H to adjust subtitle delay by 50ms. In MX Player, open Subtitle Settings > Sync to adjust.
