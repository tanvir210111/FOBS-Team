About FOBS Team

Pioneering precision Bengali subtitles for global movies and drama series.

Our Mission & Vision

FOBS Team is a dedicated subtitling collective dedicated to bridging the language barrier for Bengali cinephiles worldwide. We create and curate high-accuracy Bengali (বাংলা) subtitles for international movies, Korean Dramas (K-Drama), Korean Movies (K-Movie), Chinese Dramas (C-Drama), Hollywood blockbusters, and world cinema.

Our goal is to ensure that audiences can immerse themselves into world stories with nuanced, natural, and contextually rich Bengali dialogue rather than mechanical word-for-word machine translation.

Subtitling Standards & Principles

  • Contextual Translation: Capturing emotions, humor, idioms, and plot nuances in natural Bengali.
  • Accurate Frame Timing: Frame-accurate subtitle sync calibrated for modern 23.976, 24, 25, and 29.97 FPS digital web prints.
  • Clear Typography: Designed for high readability on smart TVs, mobile screens, and media players (VLC, MX Player, PotPlayer).
  • Community Accessibility: Free distribution for movie enthusiasts and direct community engagement on Telegram.
