Fair use, copyright notice, and community guidelines.

1. Nature of the Website

FOBS Subtitles is a non-commercial, community-driven platform created by language enthusiasts to provide high-quality Bengali text translations (subtitles) for films and television programs.

2. Intellectual Property & Non-Hosting Policy

This website does not host, upload, or store unauthorized digital media, video files, or copyrighted film streams on its servers. All video trailers are embedded via official YouTube embeds.

Subtitle files (.srt) represent independent educational and fan-translated linguistic interpretations created for accessibility and language comprehension purposes.
